Training material for the recognizer

Every dictation stores an audio + text pair. Ones marked bad are left out of training; ones you corrected go in with your own text.

Add your own word

A product, a project, a person — anything you say that the recognizer mangles. Several at once: separate by comma or newline. They go to the top of the list, because only the first ~45 terms are sent to the recognizer as a hint.

Replacements

What it heard → what is correct, separated by a TAB. Grows on its own; you can edit it.

Terms

Your words. The top ~45 are sent to the recognizer as a hint, so put what you say most often first.

Your own-language words

The corrector may not turn these into English terms: «сессию» never becomes «session».

Corrector model

The second stage: it puts punctuation and capitals back, writes your terms correctly and restores question marks. Runs locally in LM Studio. Pick any chat model you have loaded there — the choice is saved and survives a restart. Recognition itself does not depend on this.
